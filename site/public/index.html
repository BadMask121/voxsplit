Split lead and backing vocals.
On your Mac.

VoxSplit separates a song’s lead vocal from its backing vocals, or the vocals from the instrumental, with AI models running on your Mac’s Neural Engine and GPU. Nothing is uploaded.

How it works

  5. Lead Backing BS-RoFormer (Best) or MDX Karaoke (Fast)

Lead & Backing

Two models in a row: the first pulls the vocals out of the mix, the second splits the lead from the harmonies and doubles. Extra passes clean up what’s left.

Vocal Remover

Vocals and instrumental from any song. A three-minute track takes about 15 seconds on an M2 Max.

Preview, then drag

Play every stem together, mute any one of them, then drag the files straight into your project. The stems add back up to the original.

Install

One installer puts the app, the FL Studio plugin and the voxsplit command on your Mac. They share one copy of the models (about 330 MB).

With the installer

  1. Download VoxSplit-0.1.0.pkg and open it.
  2. macOS says it can’t verify the developer, because VoxSplit isn’t notarized yet. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway.
  3. Click through the installer and enter your password.

From Terminal

Skips the Open Anyway step.

Everything (asks for your password)

curl -fsSL https://voxsplit.triplejmusic.art/install.sh | bash

Only the command-line tool, no password

curl -fsSL https://voxsplit.triplejmusic.art/install.sh | bash -s -- --cli

Setup

Mac app

  1. Open VoxSplit from Applications.
  2. Drop a song on it, or click to choose one. WAV, AIFF, FLAC, MP3 and M4A all work.
  3. Pick a tool. Lead & Backing has two qualities: Best (cleanest) and Fast. Vocal Remover gives vocals and instrumental.
  4. Click Separate. The window shows how long it will take. The stems are saved to ~/Music/VoxSplit/<song name>.
  5. Press ▶ to hear all the stems together, use each stem’s speaker button to mute it, and drag a stem into any app to use it.

The very first separation takes about a minute longer while macOS prepares the models for your Mac. After that, they start straight away.

FL Studio

  1. Open FL Studio. If VoxSplit isn’t in your plugin list yet, go to Options → Manage plugins and click Find more plugins.
  2. Open the Mixer, pick an empty insert and add VoxSplit from the effect slots.
  3. Drop a song on the plugin and click Separate. Your project keeps playing while it works.
  4. Preview plays through that mixer insert. When it sounds right, drag Lead vocals or Backing vocals into the Playlist.

The VST3 and Audio Unit versions should work in other hosts too. VoxSplit is tested in FL Studio 2026.

Command line

# Lead and backing vocals, saved to ~/Music/VoxSplit/song
voxsplit separate song.mp3

# Faster model, three refinement passes, then show the files
voxsplit separate song.mp3 --quality fast --passes 3 --reveal

# Vocals and instrumental
voxsplit separate song.mp3 --tool vocal-remover

# Hear the stems together, or mute one
voxsplit play ~/Music/VoxSplit/song
voxsplit play ~/Music/VoxSplit/song --mute backing

# How long will it take?
voxsplit estimate song.mp3
OptionWhat it does
--toollead-backing (default) or vocal-remover
--qualitybest (BS-RoFormer, default) or fast (MDX Karaoke)
--passesRefinement passes, 1–5. Each one pulls more backing out of the lead.
--vocal-passesVocal passes, 0–5. 0 means the file is already vocals only.
--out-dirWhere to save the stems.
--bit-depth32f (default), 24 or 16
--revealOpen the output folder in Finder when done.
--format jsonOne JSON event per line, for scripts. This is the default when the output is piped.

Run voxsplit --help or voxsplit separate --help for everything else. Ctrl-C stops a run and leaves no partial files.

Questions

Why does macOS say it can’t verify VoxSplit?

VoxSplit isn’t notarized by Apple yet, which needs a paid developer account. Click Open Anyway in System Settings → Privacy & Security once, or install from Terminal, which skips that step. The source code is public on GitHub, and the Links section above has the installer’s SHA-256.

Is my music uploaded anywhere?

No. Separation runs entirely on your Mac and works offline. The only download is the installer itself.

Which Macs does it run on?

Macs with Apple silicon (M1 or later) running macOS 14 Sonoma or later, with about 1 GB of free space. Intel Macs and Windows aren’t supported yet.

How long does a song take?

For a 3:10 song on an M2 Max: about 2 minutes with Lead & Backing on Best, about 1 minute on Fast, and about 15 seconds with the Vocal Remover. More passes take longer. VoxSplit shows its own estimate before you start, and it learns from each run on your Mac.

What do I get?

WAV files at the song’s own sample rate (32-bit float by default) in ~/Music/VoxSplit/<song name>: lead_vocals.wav and backing_vocals.wav, or vocals.wav and instrumental.wav. Each pair adds back up to the original.

How do I uninstall it?

Run this in Terminal. Your stems in ~/Music/VoxSplit are kept.

curl -fsSL https://voxsplit.triplejmusic.art/install.sh | bash -s -- --uninstall
Whose models does it use?

VoxSplit runs models made by the separation community: Kim Vocal 2 by Kimberley Jensen and UVR-MDX-NET Karaoke 2, both from the Ultimate Vocal Remover project, and the BS-RoFormer karaoke model by frazer-becruily, converted using ZFTurbo’s BS-RoFormer code. The models belong to their authors. VoxSplit’s own code is MIT licensed.
